Targets

A target has a base kind (see Admin → Plugins), the attributes of that kind, and extensions of other kinds. Each endpoint (a port that MAST connects to) is checked by a certificate authority, a pinned certificate or host key, or not at all. When the address or a port changes, a pin must be reviewed before MAST connects again. mast targets does the same.
